## VAL-CAP-004 Failure Detail

### What worked
- Portal picker appeared and operator granted "Share screen"
- `capture.sessionReady` received: session `pw-session-0000-490323-1779146807898`, compositor=pipewire
- Capture ran for ~60s with 61 diagnostics samples
- Drop rate: 0.000000 (gate: ≤ 0.001) — **PASS**
- Diagnostic threshold "at least 1 sample": 61 received — **PASS**

### What failed

**Gate 1: cadence mean (ISS-001)**
- Required: `±0.5% of 0.00 fps`
- Observed: `105.328 fps`
- Root cause: Portal returned `fps_num=0, fps_den=1` (PipeWire variable-rate). Harness computes target as `fps_num/fps_den = 0fps`. The gate checks observed mean against 0fps band — no real capture can pass this.
- Owner: validation harness (validation/drivers.ts)
- Issue: ISS-001

**Gate 2: encoder.selected backend (ISS-002)**
- Required: `nvenc`
- Observed: `(not received)`
- Root cause: `encoder.probeResult` shows nvenc=`not-implemented-yet` and libx264=`not-implemented-yet` with `follow_up_ticket=T-017a`. No encoder backend is implemented yet; no `encoder.selected` event will be emitted until T-017a lands.
- Owner: helper/encoder
- Issue: ISS-002

### Additional observations
- Captured format was 4K (3840×2160 XR24), not 1080p — the portal delivered the monitor's native resolution. Row title says "1080p60" but the monitor is 4K.
- `capture.sessionReady` and `capture.startStream` both succeeded.
