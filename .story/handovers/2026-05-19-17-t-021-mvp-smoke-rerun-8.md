## Conclusion

**RED-BOTH** — Both cadence and drop rate fail. This is the second consecutive controlled rerun at 1920x1080@60 with VRR disabled, producing nearly identical results. The ~54 fps PipeWire delivery rate and startup drop burst are confirmed persistent environment behavior, not transient.

### Root Cause Assessment

1. **Cadence (~54 fps):** PipeWire screencast portal delivers frames at ~54 fps via variable-rate stream (fps_num=0) even with display mode at 1920x1080@60. This is a compositor/PipeWire source scheduling issue outside the application's capture pipeline. The helper receives and processes every frame delivered — it cannot produce 60 fps from a source that delivers 54.

2. **Startup drops (19 frames):** All drops occur in the first sample/second during NVENC initialization. After the first second, zero drops for the remaining 59 seconds. This is a startup transient, not a steady-state issue.

### Next-Pass Routing

Per close-out rules: **route cadence first**, because steady-state drop cannot be judged honestly through a slow source.

**Recommended next pass:** T-016a planning/implementation for PipeWire framerate hint / 60fps stream-param default. The helper's PipeWire stream setup should request an explicit framerate from the compositor rather than accepting variable-rate delivery. This is the most direct path to resolving the cadence gap.

**After cadence is resolved:** Evaluate whether the startup drop burst (19 frames in first second) warrants:
- NVENC prewarm / warmup period exclusion
- Startup-window drop tolerance policy
- Absolute-count-based drop allowance (e.g., ≤20 drops in first 2 seconds)
