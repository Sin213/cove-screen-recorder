## 14. Cadence Verdict

**FAIL** — Cadence spread gate fails.

### Failing gate
```
name:     cadence spread ≤ 6.0 fps (variable-rate compositor variance)
observed: 7.000
required: ≤ 6.0
passed:   false
```

### Mean gate (passes)
```
name:     cadence mean in variable-rate range [51.00..61.20] fps (nominal source=row-config)
observed: 54.983
required: 51.00..61.20
passed:   true
```

### Root cause of spread failure

Both `spreadFps` and `meanFps` are computed from ALL 60 samples (including the warmup sample at 61 fps):
- All 60 samples: min=54, max=61, spread=**7** fps; mean=3299/60=**54.983** (matches reported)
- Effective 59 samples (post-warmup): min=54, max=56, spread=**2** fps; mean=3238/59=**54.881**

The warmup exclusion (`dropWarmupSamples=1`) is correctly applied to drop rate, but both cadence statistics (`meanFps` and `spreadFps`) are computed from the full sample array. Evidence: the reported mean of 54.983 matches the all-sample mean exactly (not the post-warmup mean of 54.881). The mean gate passes with either scope since both 54.983 and 54.881 are within [51.00..61.20]. The spread gate fails with all-sample scope (7 > 6.0) and would pass with post-warmup scope (2 ≤ 6.0).

**This is a calculation scope bug in `validation/drivers.ts`: both `spreadFps` and `meanFps` must be computed from the effective (post-warmup) sample slice. Apply warmup exclusion consistently to all cadence inputs, the same way it is already applied to drop rate.**
