### Root Cause

PipeWire delivers ~54-55 fps via variable-rate stream (fps_num=0) even with the display mode set to 1920x1080@60. This appears to be a compositor/PipeWire source delivery rate issue — the screencast portal does not guarantee frame delivery at the display refresh rate. The initial 17-frame drop burst is likely startup transient.

This is a host/compositor-layer issue, not an encoder or capture pipeline bug.

## Recommendations

1. **File owner-layer issue** for PipeWire variable-rate delivery: KDE/PipeWire screencast portal delivers ~55 fps instead of 60 fps at 1920x1080@60, with fps_num=0 (variable rate). This is outside the application's control.

2. **Evaluate threshold tolerance** for the 1080p60-nvenc tier: the current threshold requires exactly 0% drops. A small tolerance (e.g., 0.5-1%) might be appropriate for variable-rate PipeWire streams, but this is a product/validation policy decision that requires explicit approval.

3. **Consider nominalFps=55 investigation**: the consistent ~55 fps delivery at a 60 Hz output suggests a KDE/PipeWire compositor scheduling or vsync issue that could be investigated at the compositor level.

4. **Do not start T-010c** until T-021 is resolved.
