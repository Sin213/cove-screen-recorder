**Date:** 2026-05-18

This addendum corrects the original rerun-3 handover which listed only ISS-001 and ISS-002 as VAL-CAP-004 blockers. ISS-003 was filed after the handover was written and must be included in the blocking issue list.

## Correction: VAL-CAP-004 has three underlying blockers, not two

The original handover's VAL-CAP-004 failure section listed:
- ISS-001 (cadence harness bug)
- ISS-002 (encoder not-implemented-yet)

ISS-003 was filed subsequently and must be treated as a co-equal blocker:

| ID | Title | Severity | Owner Layer |
|----|-------|----------|-------------|
| ISS-001 | VAL-CAP-004 cadence gate fails against fps_num=0 (variable-rate PipeWire) | high | validation |
| ISS-002 | VAL-CAP-004 encoder.selected gate fails — encoder backends not-implemented-yet (T-017a) | high | helper/encoder |
| ISS-003 | VAL-CAP-004 capture workload mismatch — portal delivered 4K/variable-fps instead of 1080p60 | high | capture+validation |

## Why ISS-003 is required to unblock VAL-CAP-004

ISS-001 fixes the harness cadence target computation (fps_num=0 → uses 0fps as target).
But even after ISS-001 is resolved, the cadence gate would use a 60fps target — and the actual
capture still ran at ~105fps from a 4K (3840×2160) monitor. The gate would still fail.

ISS-003 tracks the question of whether the portal/PipeWire capture can be negotiated to 1080p60,
or whether the row thresholds must be reconfigured to match the actual hardware.

Fixing ISS-001 alone is insufficient. Both ISS-001 and ISS-003 must be resolved (along with ISS-002
for the encoder gate) before VAL-CAP-004 can pass.

## Ready-for-T-010c verdict remains RED

Three confirmed defects (ISS-001, ISS-002, ISS-003). T-010a and T-021 remain open.
